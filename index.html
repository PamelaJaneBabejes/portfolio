System Analyst & IT Project Assistant Approach

My structured approach for each role, highlighting key responsibilities and steps.

System Analyst Approach

Meet with stakeholders to capture functional and non-functional requirements for clear, actionable documentation.

Create SRS diagrams and workflow visualizations to clearly map processes and data interactions.

Draft system architecture, interface design, and database structures to ensure efficient implementation.

Prepare clear and detailed documentation for developers, QA teams, and stakeholders.

Regularly update stakeholders and ensure alignment between business needs and technical solutions.

IT Project Assistant Approach

Assist in planning, tracking, and organizing project activities efficiently.

Maintain project logs, reports, and documents for transparency and reference.

Track project milestones and assist project managers in meeting deadlines.

Liaise with team members and stakeholders to clarify requirements and share updates.

Help resolve minor issues and escalate larger concerns to the project manager promptly.
